# Recording a call, and getting the text

The one paragraph both the warm lesson and the sales lesson quote. Every transcriber here runs on the laptop or the phone; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**A 20-minute warm call.** Zoom, free, hosted from the desktop app (a phone host records nothing). Press Record. When the call ends, drop the file on MacWhisper on a Mac (free, from macwhisper.com) or Buzz on Windows (free, from github.com/chidiwilliams/buzz; the installer is unsigned, so Windows shows "More info", then "Run anyway"). Set the language to the call's, export `.txt`, then paste it or give the path. In person: the phone's recorder, then the same file dropped on the same app. Free Google Meet records nothing; take the call on Zoom or in person.

**A 60-minute sales call.** Zoom Pro writes the transcript itself as a `.vtt`, once cloud recording and the audio transcript are switched on, one time. Or Cal Video or Google Meet, one-on-one, with Meetily on either laptop. An Intel Mac takes Zoom Basic's 40-minute cut instead.

**Nothing recorded** means every quote is the founder's memory, labeled `(founder's recollection · Name · date)`, and the draft offer waits for a recorded call.

**Transcript file types, both skills:** `.txt`, `.md`, `.srt`, `.vtt`.
