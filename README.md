# Warm Extract: install in 60 seconds

The part of an Execution Squad that turns a warm call into a folder. You talk to someone you already know for 20 minutes, record it, and hand this the text. It saves their words as they said them, then names the problem, what it costs them, what they pay for now, the one thing you could deliver, which model that is, and the next step. One folder per person under `squad/clients/`. When a call hands you an offer, it writes the draft into `squad/business.md` and stops for one number: the price you will say.

## What to bring

The transcript. Record the call (the lesson says how, one paragraph, for any laptop) and export it as `.txt`; `.md`, `.srt` and `.vtt` work too. No recording? Tell it the call from memory, messy, in any order. It labels those lines as your memory and takes them anyway.

## Run it

Open Claude Code in your business folder. (Downloaded this folder on its own? Drop the whole thing into `.claude/skills/`, then quit and reopen Claude Code.) Then:

- **"I talked with Jordan"** and paste the transcript, or give the path to the file.
- **"/warm-extract"** does the same.
- **"Self-interview"** when there is nobody to call. It asks you the script's 8 questions about who you were before you learned what you know now, one at a time, then asks who is standing there today that you could reach.

One person per run. 1 to 5 people, then the next part.

## What you get

`squad/clients/<name>/notes.md` for every person: their quotes, verbatim and dated, the problem, the cost, what they pay now, the idea, the model, the next step. The transcript sits next to it. Copy the strongest quote into your Outreach Sheet by hand.

When someone asked you for a named thing and put a date on the next step, `squad/business.md` gets the draft of your offer and the run stops for one number, the price you will say. Type it, open the sales lesson, and sell it to that person.

It never sends anything and it never prices anything.

## What comes next

The Winning Offer reads every folder and forges them into one offer document with one answer on it. It arrives one episode at a time. Subscribe (the link under every episode) and each new part lands in your inbox the day its episode goes live.
